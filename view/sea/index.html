The Sea Paintings

Chas Pulido paints with the canvas flat on the studio floor in San Francisco. He works it from all four sides, pouring and throwing acrylic from above, so each painting is made by moving around it as much as standing in front of it.

The Sea Paintings are nine works about the water, made over 2025 and 2026. Three of them are eight feet wide. Some are surf, some are reef and some are open water at night. The coast paintings carry the warmth of the land behind them.

Pulido came to painting without formal training and still talks about the work in plain terms. He wants it to feel joyful, and he trusts what happens when he stops steering the paint.

“When the canvas is flat, my whole body becomes part of the brush.”
Chas Pulido

The artist

Chas Pulido

b. 1997. Lives and works in San Francisco.

Pulido paints large abstract works on the floor of his studio, pouring and throwing acrylic over canvases that run up to eight feet wide. His work is collected privately, and he has sold originals directly to collectors every month since the summer of 2025.
